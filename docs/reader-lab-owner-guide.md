# Your Reader Lab: a quick guide

This is a private trial of your new magazine. You can try writing and publishing,
and see your magazine in five different designs. Nothing here is public, and
nothing you do here can break anything. Take your time.

You will need:

- the **link** we sent you;
- about 20 minutes.

## 1. Create your password (first time only)

1. Open the link we sent you. You may first be asked for the trial's access
   details. Use the ones we gave you.
2. You will see **Create your password** with your email address.
3. Choose a password of at least 14 characters. A short sentence you can remember
   works well, for example four unrelated words. **Show password** lets you check
   your typing.
4. Select **Create account**. You are signed in and taken to the **Reader Lab**.

The link works once. Next time, use the sign-in page we sent you, together with
your email and password.

If the page says to wait, wait 15 minutes and open the link again. If it still
does not work, tell us and we will send you a new link.

## 2. Take the tour

The first time you open the Reader Lab, a short tour starts by itself. Use
**Next step** to move on, or **Back** to go back. **Close tour** ends it. You can
replay it any time with **Take the tour**.

## 3. Look at the five designs

The Reader Lab shows the same magazine in five designs:

| Design | In a sentence |
| --- | --- |
| Quiet Monograph | A calm gallery catalogue with lots of white space. |
| Literary Long-read | Elegant serif type made for long reading. |
| Swiss Index | A precise, structured grid with bold type. |
| Cinematic Feature | Big images and dramatic, film-like headlines. |
| Expressive Colour | Soft letters and a colour for every story. |

On each design you can choose **View the site in this direction** (the front page)
or **Open latest story** (an article).

While you are looking at the magazine, a bar at the bottom helps you compare:

- **Previous** and **Next** switch to another design on the same page. This is the
  quickest way to compare.
- **All directions** takes you back to the list of designs.
- **Exit lab** shows the normal version.

Only you see these designs. Visitors would always see the normal version.

## 4. Write and publish a test story

Your own writing is the best test, so try a real piece if you have one.

1. Go to **Stories**, then **New story**.
2. Add a **Story title**, a **Short summary** and your text. Your work saves
   automatically while you type.
3. Add a picture with **Add image** if you like.
4. Select **Preview** to see it as a reader would.
5. When you are happy, select **Publish now**.
6. Go back to the **Reader Lab** and choose **Open latest story** on each design
   to see your story in all five.

Try it on your phone too. Designs can feel very different on a small screen.

## 5. Decide and tell us

Look at each design and ask yourself:

- Is it comfortable to read a long story?
- Do the pictures look good?
- Does it work on your phone?
- Does it feel like *your* magazine?

Then send us your favourite (and a second choice, if you have one), with a few
words on why. Notes on what you did not like help just as much.

## Good to know

- This is a trial. We may reset it later, so keep your own copy of anything you
  write here that you want to keep.
- Your older articles will be brought over for you. You do not need to copy them.
- If anything looks wrong or confusing, a screenshot and a sentence about what you
  expected is perfect.
